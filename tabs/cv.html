Governance & Policy

I am drawn to the complex intersections between global governance, migration, and climate change. My academic work explores how international institutions negotiate power, whose voices are amplified, and which frameworks guide — or fail to guide — equitable decision-making. I am particularly interested in questions of sovereignty, legal personhood, and the possibilities for building more inclusive, participatory global systems in the face of environmental and political crises.
