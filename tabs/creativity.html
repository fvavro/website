Art & Creativity

Creativity has always been central to how I understand and move through the world. Whether through painting, dance, zine-making, or writing, I seek forms of expression that connect emotion to broader ecological, social, and political narratives. I believe that art is not just a medium for reflection, but a force for imagining alternatives — spaces of joy, resistance, and transformation.
