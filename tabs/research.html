Research

I study climate governance, policy design, and environmental justice. I leverage computer science methods—data pipelines, classification, and NLP— to analyze negotiation narratives and public discourse, grounding findings in community work and policy practice.

Thesis: Narratives in UN Climate Negotiations

I analyze the evolution of sovereignty, loss & damage, and climate finance narratives using the Earth Negotiations Bulletin corpus (daily reports & summaries). Methods: structured scraping, metadata normalization, phrase-set curation, and NLP-assisted coding with human validation.
